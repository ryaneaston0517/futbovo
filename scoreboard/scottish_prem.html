Scored: every model pick on the lane graded a win or a loss, as stamped. One observation per decision, from its last pricing run before kickoff where the kickoff is recorded; a decision with no recorded kickoff keeps its last pricing run, unbounded, and each lane page counts those rows. A decision priced only after a recorded kickoff is not scored, and a push or void carries no outcome. The model's verdict decides what is endorsed; placed bets, skips and every other operator judgement are not on this page and enter no figure. Losses and PASS days are listed: no win or loss is filtered out.

Calibration is scored once per decision group (one k-outcome vector per match and market) as log-loss −ln p(winner) and Brier Σ(p − o)², lower is better, against the devigged closing market and the ln k ignorance baseline. The gap is model − market, a mean of per-match means; positive means the market was ahead. CLV is closing − entry no-vig probability over endorsed (+EV) decisions; the bar reads the mean per decision, and the t is taken over per-match means so picks sharing one match count once. The bar: at least 100 endorsed decisions over at least 60 matches with mean CLV strictly above +0.0100, and |t| of at least 1.96 over at least 60 matches for a STATISTICAL grade. Below it, a figure is directional, not a verdict. Matches needed is the match count that would give 80% power against an effect the size of the current per-match mean CLV: an order of magnitude, not a date, shown from 10 matches.
